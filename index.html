All county averages and method

We sample a 24 × 24 grid across each county’s bounding box, retain points within its boundaries, and average each point’s distance to its nearest provider anywhere in the saved California dataset. Latitude weights approximate land-area weighting; these are not population-weighted distances. Small islands may be missed. AI distances are unavailable because no provider locations were estimated.

Experimental provenance and interpretation

The response was generated October 11, 2026 using existing model knowledge, before opening project data or maps. Its county estimates remain unchanged after comparison. Download the full response, exact prompt, and metadata.

The interface does not expose an exact model identifier, temperature, seed, or top-p; those metadata fields are explicitly null. The assistant is described as Codex based on GPT-6, which is not a verified API model identifier.

The model associated large urban counties with greater absolute numbers, and Central Valley and some rural counties with higher rates and support needs. These associations may encode stereotypes. This single response cannot establish a general urban bias across models. The statistics now use sourced county classifications and total population, but testing general bias still requires repeated independent responses and clear distinctions between rates and absolute numbers.

All confidence labels are self-reported and uncalibrated. Unknown provider counts in the original birth-rate experiment are not zeros; the separate provider experiment below does not replace them. No locations were fabricated to calculate AI accessibility. Observed accessibility still uses the original area-weighted grid and straight-line distance method.

AFLP provider-count evaluation

Observed counts refer to distinct listed addresses in the September 5, 2026 directory snapshot. Counties with no listed address have observed count zero in this snapshot. Listings do not measure service capacity, eligibility, outreach from other counties, or all available support. A predicted zero is not evidence that a county has no services.

Snapshot limitation: All 18 listed addresses fall in 15 metro counties under the saved RUCC classification. None is listed in the 21 nonmetro counties. Rural omission of observed AFLP locations therefore cannot be tested here; this does not mean rural counties have no services.

Data sources

  • CDPH — Adolescent Births. Supplied CSV; 2024 single-year “Adolescent Birth Rate,” ages 15–19, total population. Original data · CDPH methodology.
  • CDPH — Adolescent Family Life Program, Where We Are. Public directory retrieved September 5, 2026; 17 addresses matched by the US Census Bureau and one street-level approximation from OpenStreetMap. Directory snapshot.
  • California Department of Technology — California County Boundaries and Identifiers. Without coastal buffers; retrieved September 5, 2026 and simplified for display.

Limitations

Adolescent birth data represent live births and do not represent every pregnant youth. Publicly listed AFLP locations indicate where programs are located, but do not directly measure actual service accessibility or capacity. The directory snapshot and birth data describe different dates; this map does not establish causal relationships.

Following CDPH’s suppression rule, rates and birth counts with a numerator below 10 are shown as “No data,” including zero-coded rows in the supplied export. This dashboard uses the latest single year available in that file; CDPH’s own county dashboard uses three-year aggregates. Geocoded mailing addresses are approximate and may differ from service delivery sites. The Madera point is a street-level approximation on Sunrise Avenue, not a verified building location.
